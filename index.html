Work in Progress

  • Incentives, Effort, and Gender in Professional Tennis (with Prakarsh Singh). Measures effort responses to changes in prize structure using match-level performance and ability data with a difference-in-differences design.
  • Employment Protection Design and Firm Litigation: Evidence from Gujarat. Uses spatial variation in labour law within Gujarat to test whether the design of employment protection legislation shapes firm litigation.
  • Dismantling Dismissal Protection: Evidence from Indian Plant-Level Data (with Prakarsh Singh and Saurabh Gupta). Uses plant-level Annual Survey of Industries panel data and staggered difference-in-differences across reforming states to estimate how removing dismissal protections and increasing severance reshapes employment and wages across firm sizes.
  • Front-Loaded vs. Back-Loaded Incentives: Experimental Evidence. Lab experiments testing theoretical predictions on incentive timing and its effects on effort, persistence, and performance.
